How this works

You sign each letter for about ten seconds while the camera watches. Then you get a small file to send to the person who built this.

What is in the file

  • For every captured frame, 63 numbers that describe the shape of your hand.
  • The letter you were signing, a random session code, a timestamp, and which hand it was.
  • No video, no photos, no name.

Nothing is uploaded. The file stays on your device until you choose to send it yourself.

Before you start

  • Only record letters you know well. If you are unsure of one, skip it. A wrong shape makes the recogniser worse for everyone.
  • A plain background and decent light help. Either hand is fine.
  • If you are under 18, ask a parent or teacher first.
